🔥.THE "ASSET REVIEW" PITCH (Recommended Approach)

This is the strongest approach to use with Premium (Solitaire / Privy / Privy+) clients. Do not individually pitch 7 loans.

Customer Pitch

“Sir/Ma’am, as your Relationship Manager, one exercise I'd like to do once every year is a quick liability and asset review.

I'll understand what assets you own, what loans you currently have, what rates you're paying and where your future funding requirements may come up.

For example, if you have a home loan elsewhere, we can evaluate a balance transfer. If you have a debt-free property, we can assess the liquidity available against it. If you run a business, we can look at working-capital requirements or an OD instead of you liquidating investments whenever there is a cash-flow gap.

The idea is not to increase your borrowing. It's to make sure that whenever you need financing, you're using the most appropriate source of capital.

I'll do the evaluation and only recommend something if I genuinely see a benefit for you.”

The Key RM Mindset

Don't ask: “Sir, do you need a loan?”

Ask: “What major financial requirement do you see coming up over the next 6–12 months?”

Then diagnose:

Requirement → Cash-flow → Existing liability → Available collateral → Appropriate structure → Kotak solution.

That is the difference between selling an asset product and doing a relationship-led lending conversation.

3.OD — OVERDRAFT

This needs to be pitched differently from a normal loan.

Customer Pitch

“Sir/Ma’am, if your requirement is not a fixed ₹X crore today, but rather access to liquidity whenever required, I would actually suggest that we evaluate an overdraft structure instead of taking the entire loan upfront.

The key advantage is flexibility—you have a sanctioned limit available, you draw what you actually need, and repayment can be made as your cash flow comes in.

This can be particularly useful for business owners or customers with irregular cash flows, seasonal requirements or temporary liquidity gaps.

So instead of borrowing ₹1 crore and paying interest on the entire amount from day one, we can evaluate whether an OD structure is more appropriate for your actual cash-flow pattern.”

Key Benefits

  • Sanctioned credit limit available when required
  • Draw only as required, subject to facility terms
  • Useful for short-term liquidity gaps
  • Flexible withdrawal and repayment
  • Particularly relevant for variable/seasonal cash flows
  • Can be structured against property for eligible customers
  • Kotak also offers Digi OD for eligible business customers, with unsecured limits advertised up to ₹15 lakh and paperless processing
  • LAP can also be structured as an OD facility

Key Question

“Does your cash requirement come in one large requirement, or do you typically need ₹10–20 lakh here, ₹30 lakh there depending on the month?”

If variable → OD conversation.
